# Country and money reference notes

Reviewed 2026-11-08 against public reference documentation. No customer/provider account or hosted application database was accessed. Names and the seven regional groupings follow the requested PaceMate product coverage; regional groups are not represented as an ISO geographic standard.

- ISO country codes: [ISO 3166 Online Browsing Platform](https://www.iso.org/obp/ui/#search/code/) and [Unicode CLDR region identifiers](https://www.unicode.org/reports/tr35/#unicode_region_subtag). The application country catalogue contains exactly the requested 106 alpha-2 codes. Country locales are supported application formatting locales, not assertions about official languages.
- ISO currency designations and minor units: [SIX ISO 4217 Maintenance Agency](https://www.six-group.com/en/products-services/financial-information/market-reference-data/data-standards.html). ISO precision is stored explicitly; CLDR cash rounding must not replace accounting precision. The site's full XML feed could not be read by the browsing tool; country/currency mappings were manually curated, cross-checked with [Unicode CLDR supplemental currency data](https://github.com/unicode-org/cldr/blob/main/common/supplemental/supplementalData.xml) and covered by the catalogue validation tests. This is not an automated feed synchronization job.
- Bulgaria uses EUR from 2026-01-01: [European Central Bank](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/press/pr/date/2026/html/ecb.pr260101~c830245e42.en.html) and SIX amendment 180. BGN is available only for original historical amounts.
- Sierra Leone uses SLE with two minor units: [SIX amendment 171](https://www.six-group.com/dam/download/financial-information/data-center/iso-currrency/amendments/dl_currency_iso_amendment_171.pdf) and [amendment 172](https://www.six-group.com/dam/download/financial-information/data-center/iso-currrency/amendments/dl-currency-iso-amendment-172.pdf). SLL is historical and is not silently converted.
- Zimbabwe Gold is ZWG with two minor units: [SIX amendment 177](https://www.six-group.com/dam/download/financial-information/data-center/iso-currrency/amendments/dl-currency-iso-amendment-177.pdf). Do not substitute the obsolete ZWL code. Zimbabwe also has a multi-currency environment; the configured local default is not a declaration of exclusive legal tender or payment-provider availability.
- Country/currency defaults are not exclusive legal-tender assertions: for example, Liberia defaults to LRD, Panama to PAB and Zimbabwe to ZWG, while other currencies may also be used there. Hong Kong, Taiwan and Puerto Rico have independent alpha-2 entries as requested. XCD/XPF are display currencies even though no additional country/territory coverage was invented for them.
- Formatting uses the runtime's `Intl.NumberFormat`/CLDR symbols and grouping with explicitly controlled ISO fractional digits. BigInt integer/fraction assembly prevents IEEE-754 price arithmetic. A runtime without a newer localized glyph can show its ISO code rather than an invented symbol.
- Provider amount representations: [Stripe currencies](https://docs.stripe.com/currencies). Stripe ISK/UGX representations use two decimal places although their ISO precision is zero; fractional provider values are rejected. MGA charges use whole units while ISO accounting precision is two. Stripe three-decimal charges require a zero final digit. No general Stripe currency-acceptance claim follows from the display registry.
- Provider lifecycle and security: [subscription webhooks](https://docs.stripe.com/billing/subscriptions/webhooks), [Checkout Sessions](https://docs.stripe.com/api/checkout/sessions/create), [event retrieval](https://docs.stripe.com/api/events/retrieve) and [charge dispute retrieval](https://docs.stripe.com/api/disputes/list). Outbound APIs are pinned; account-specific acceptance and full live/sandbox flows still require the hosted checklist.

Re-review the catalogue and provider rules when approving a new market or currency adoption. Adding a displayed currency does not approve a price or enable checkout. Keep withdrawn codes only for original historical records.
